The collection

Estate Reserva

A collection of expressive wines, made to meet every kind of story.

01Sauvignon Blanc↗ Citrus, tangerine, lime and tropical fruits. Perfect for a pizza night. 02Carmenere↗Full bodied, delicately spicy, and smooth tannins. Perfect with pasta dishes such as lamb ragu. 03Cabernet Sauvignon↗Silky and smooth. Red fruit and black fruit, vanilla and chocolate. Great to pair with your Sunday Roast. 04Chardonnay↗Fresh, crisp, lively, and delicious. Perfect to complement your picnics. 05Merlot↗Fruity and smooth. Fresh, herbal and balsamic notes. Try it with cheese platter, simple pasta dishes. 06Pinot Grigio↗Fresh, fruity nose which marries the classic pear and apple fragrances with elegant hints of floral and citrus. 07Pinot Noir↗Fragrant with red fruits, flowers and balsamic. Try it with lamb chops and grilled vegetables. 08Syrah↗Smooth and spicy. Aromas of fresh red fruits and sweet spices, the perfect wine for your BBQ.
